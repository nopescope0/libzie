About

libzie
Current version —

A small archive that runs locally on your computer and organizes your lecture notes, slides and PDFs by course.

Updates

New version
—

When checked, libzie looks for a new version every time it starts, downloads it if there is one and restarts. This setting is saved immediately.

Privacy: no data is collected. Your files stay on your computer; only the weather (city name, to Open-Meteo) and the update check (to GitHub) go online.
